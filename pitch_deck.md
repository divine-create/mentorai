# MentorAI Pitch Deck - AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon

## Slide 1: Title Slide
**MentorAI: The Future of Interactive Voice Tutoring**
*Empowering students with real-time, voice-first AI education.*
- **Built for:** AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon
- **Powered by:** AssemblyAI Real-time Speech-to-Text

---

## Slide 2: The Problem
**Passive Learning is Broken**
- Traditional e-learning is one-sided, text-heavy, and passive, leading to low engagement and high dropout rates.
- Students lack immediate, conversational feedback and personalized guidance when they get stuck.
- Current text-based AI chatbots lack the natural, frictionless interaction of a real human tutor, making learning feel like a chore rather than a conversation.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (MentorAI)
**An Interactive, Voice-First AI Tutor**
- **Listens:** Uses AssemblyAI's Real-time WebSocket API to transcribe student questions instantly and accurately.
- **Understands & Responds:** Analyzes the context and replies with personalized, markdown-rich educational content (including code blocks and formatting).
- **Speaks:** Reads responses out loud automatically, creating a seamless, hands-free conversational loop.
- **Result:** Transforms passive reading into an engaging, two-way tutoring session.

---

## Slide 4: Application of Technology (Our Stack)
**Built for Speed and Accuracy**
- **AssemblyAI:** The core of our voice agent. We utilize the Real-Time STT API over WebSockets for sub-second transcription, ensuring the tutor never misses a beat.
- **Next.js & React:** Provides a lightning-fast, responsive UI with real-time chat bubbles.
- **Supabase:** Manages our backend database, secure connections, and session states.
- **Browser-Native TTS:** Provides frictionless text-to-speech output without extra latency, allowing AssemblyAI to shine as the primary input engine.

---

## Slide 5: Business Value
**Scalable, Personalized Education**
- **Market Size:** The global EdTech market is booming. MentorAI brings premium, 1-on-1 tutoring to the masses at a fraction of the cost.
- **B2B & B2C Potential:** Can be licensed to universities and existing e-learning platforms, or offered directly to students as a subscription.
- **Accessibility:** Voice-first interaction makes learning more accessible for younger students, visually impaired users, and auditory learners.

---

## Slide 6: Originality & Demo Highlights
**Not Just Another Chatbot**
- **Multimodal Learning:** Seamlessly blends audio conversation with rich visual context (code blocks, text UI). 
- **Proactive Engagement:** The AI doesn't just wait; it auto-starts sessions with an engaging vocal greeting to instantly hook the learner.
- **Frictionless UI:** Clean, high-contrast interface designed specifically for readability and focus.

---

## Slide 7: Future Roadmap
**Where We Go Next**
- **Multilingual Support:** Leveraging AssemblyAI's multilingual models to tutor students in their native languages.
- **Emotional Intelligence:** Analyzing vocal tone to detect student frustration and adjust the teaching style automatically.
- **Custom Curricula:** Allowing teachers to upload their own course materials for MentorAI to teach from.
